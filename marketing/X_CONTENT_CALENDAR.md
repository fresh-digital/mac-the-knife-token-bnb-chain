## Doctrine & guardrails (read once)

**Distribution levers** (grounded in Fresh Digital's `[[content-distribution-optimization]]`: optimize
timing · channel · format · messaging · frequency; move from "publish and hope" to deliberate cadence).
The brain has no crypto-specific launch playbook, so the *structure* below adapts that general doctrine
to an X-first token launch — treat every number as a testable hypothesis, not gospel (`[[systematic-
testing-framework]]`).

**Voice guardrails (non-negotiable):**
- Menace aims at the **archetype** — "the rug," "the hidden hand," "scammers" — **never** a named
  person or specific real wallet. No @-ing targets. No claims of surveillance, framing, or intrusion.
- Every threat cashes out to a **checkable fact** (self-renouncing, no mint, no blacklist, 0 tax).
- Any financial-sounding post carries a plain **not-financial-advice / DYOR** line. Never promise price
  or returns.
- Cold, declarative, few words. The account is a myth, not a person to argue with.

## The five phases

### Phase 0 — GENESIS (Days 1–7): mystery, zero product
Cryptic only. Build the face before you sell the coin. No token address, no price talk.
- **Anchor:** the key art + tagline "The MAC is the machine's face. The Knife removes it."
- Seed the hex. Seed the broken mask. Seed the countdown as a symbol, not yet a real timer.

### Phase 1 — THE LEGEND (Days 8–14): reveal the myth
Decode the name. Tell hakky's story. Turn intrigue into a world people want to belong to.

### Phase 2 — THE PROOF (Days 15–21): earn trust before launch
Pivot from myth to mechanism. Introduce the Deadhand Cut, the guarantees, testnet, "verify don't
trust." This is where a skeptic becomes a believer.

### Phase 3 — THE CUT (Days 22–24): launch
Deploy, liquidity, open trading, LP lock, the real countdown begins. Maximum signal density.

### Phase 4 — OWNERLESS (Days 25–30): prove it
Watch the timer die on-chain. Renounce. Lock proof. Hand the myth to the Collective.

## What to measure (not just followers)

Per `[[systematic-testing-framework]]`, treat cadence/format as experiments:
- **Saves + reposts** over likes (intent to return / spread).
- **Profile→link CTR** during Proof/Cut phases (verification clicks = trust working).
- Which **pillar** drives follows (double down on the winner in weeks 2–4).
- Best posting **window** by engagement — lock it in after week 1.

## Compliance line

No targeting or threatening real individuals or wallets. No claims of surveillance, framing, or system
intrusion. No price/return promises; disclaimers on anything financial. If a real anti-scam angle is
ever wanted, use *informational* public scam-address awareness (the above-board cousin), never a
threat campaign.
