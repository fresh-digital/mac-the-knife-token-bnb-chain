# MAC the KNIFE — Character & Visual Guide
*Design bible for artists, animators, and image-gen. Pairs with [CODEX.md](CODEX.md).*

---

## 1. Design north star

One line drives every visual: **the one who stopped hiding.** Anonymous covered its face; hakky's
mask is *broken* — half of it gone, an eye showing through. He's a ghost who chose to be seen. Every
frame should carry that tension: menace + transparency. Not "scary hacker in a hoodie." **A blade held
in an open, empty hand.**

---

## 2. Character: hakky / the Knife

**Silhouette (most important — must read at thumbnail size):** hooded figure, one arm forward holding
a glowing blade *low and relaxed*, the other hand open and empty at his side (palm visible — the "no
hidden hand" tell). The hood + the shard-of-light knife should be identifiable as a pure black
silhouette on gold.

**The mask (the relic):** a cracked, pale Legion-style mask, but **only half remains** — fused to the
right side of his face like salvage. The intact half is serene; the exposed half is just a person. He
kept the relic; he didn't hide behind it.

**The blade:** not steel. A **shard of the shattered BNB hexagon** — molten-gold, glitching at the
edges into hex digits and MAC-address fragments (`6D:61:63…`). When it "cuts," it doesn't draw blood;
it **deletes a face** (a fingerprint dissolving into particles).

**The hands:** always show at least one **open, empty palm.** This is the character's entire thesis in
body language. Never both hands hidden.

**Palette on-character:** near-black cloak, skin in cold grey-blue, everything lit by the gold of the
blade. The only saturated color in frame is BNB gold.

**Avoid the clichés:** no green "Matrix" code, no ski-mask-and-laptop, no anonymous-mask-worn-whole
(that's the *old* Legion, the thing he broke from), no red. The gold is the discipline.

## 3. Iconography — the glyph system

Five marks, reusable everywhere (favicons, stickers, cut-transitions, PFPs):

| Glyph | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **The Knife** | the removal | primary logo mark; the "cut" |
| **The Shattered Hexagon** | BNB, breaking | frames, backgrounds, the blade's origin |
| **The Cut Fingerprint** | identity removed | privacy motif, the "disappear" beat |
| **The MAC String** | the machine's face | `6D:61:63:74:68:65` as a recurring texture |
| **The Countdown** | the Deadhand | a ticking `72:00:00` — the honesty mechanic, visualized |

The **open empty hand** is the sixth, quieter glyph — use it wherever "trust / transparency" is the message.

## 4. Color & type

**Color (exact):**
- BNB Gold — primary: `#F0B90B`  · highlight: `#F3BA2F`
- Void — background: `#0B0E11`  · panel: `#181A20`
- Bone — text/mask: `#EAECEF`
- Rule: **gold is a scalpel, not a paint bucket.** Mostly void and bone; gold only on the blade, the
  key word, the countdown. Scarcity of gold = menace.

**Type:**
- **Display** (MAC the KNIFE): a heavy condensed grotesque, distressed/stencilled — think weathered
  industrial paint (as on the key art). All-caps.
- **Technical layer** (hex, addresses, `status()` readouts): a monospace (e.g. a JetBrains/IBM Plex
  Mono class face). This is the "machine's voice."
- **Body**: a clean neutral sans for litepaper/site.
- Signature move: set the *hook word* — REMOVES, CUT, EXPIRE — in gold; the rest bone.

---

## 5. Logo lockups

1. **Primary wordmark:** `MAC the KNIFE` — "the" small and lowercase between two heavy caps words,
   the K of KNIFE nicked/sliced.
2. **Mark:** shattered hexagon with the gold blade through it (the poster's core image). Square, works
   as PFP/favicon.
3. **Signature lockup:** the wordmark above the MAC string `6D:61:63:74:68:65`, monospace — the "face"
   under the name.
4. **Ticker tag:** `$KNIFE` in mono, gold `$`.

---

## 6. Motion (for video / X clips)

- **The Cut transition:** gold blade swipes screen → frame shatters into hex particles → next scene.
- **The Countdown ident:** `72:00:00` ticking in mono; on `00:00:00` the word **OWNERLESS** stamps.
- **The Moritat glitch:** UI/devices flicker; eight notes of the ballad implied by a waveform that
  briefly forms a knife shape. (Pure vibe — no real audio capability implied.)
- Keep cuts hard and short. Silence and black are tools. Restraint reads as confidence.

## 7. AI image-gen prompt seeds

Starting points (tune per tool; keep the "open empty hand" and "half-broken mask" to stay on-model):

- *"Cinematic key art, hooded vigilante in near-black cloak, half a cracked pale mask fused to the
  right of his face, one hand open and empty palm-out, other hand holding a glowing molten-gold blade
  made of shattered hexagon shards glitching into hex code, pure black background, single gold light
  source, high contrast, matte, no text — moody, restrained."*
- *"Logo mark, a shattered geometric hexagon pierced by a golden knife, flat vector, gold #F0B90B on
  void black #0B0E11, sharp, iconic, thumbnail-legible."*
- *"A dissolving fingerprint turning into gold particles and hexadecimal digits, dark background,
  minimal, symbolic of identity removed."*
- *"Distressed industrial stencil title card reading MAC the KNIFE, weathered gold and bone paint on
  black metal, cinematic."*

Always append: `no green code, no red, no ski mask, gold used sparingly`.

---

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do:** show the empty hand · keep gold scarce · use the monospace machine-voice for anything the
contract "says" · let black space breathe · keep the mask broken.

**Don't:** whole Legion mask (that's the villains) · green matrix rain · neon overload · gore (he
removes *faces/identities*, not people) · imply real surveillance or real-world weapons — the menace is
myth + the verifiable timer, never a claim of reaching a real person.
