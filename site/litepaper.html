§02 · The Mechanic

The Deadhand Cut

The deployer gets a short list of honest launch chores and nothing else — all of them on a countdown.

  1. 01

    Deploy mints the whole supply once. There is no mint function afterward. Supply can never grow.

  2. 02

    The window opens (72h). The deployer may only open trading once, set an optional anti-snipe cap before launch, and exempt the liquidity pair.

  3. 03

    The window closes. Every KNIFE-specific owner power reverts permanently. No mint, no freeze, no dial — no hand left on any token lever.

  4. 04

    Liveness is guaranteed. After the deadline all transfers pass by construction. The token cannot be frozen forever, even if the deployer vanishes.
